# ECOTOX Knowledgebase: A Comprehensive Guide for Environmental Researchers and Drug Development Professionals

## Summary
The ECOTOX Knowledgebase, managed by the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), is the world's largest curated repository of single-chemical ecotoxicity data. This comprehensive platform integrates over one million test records covering more than 12,000 chemicals and 13,000 aquatic and terrestrial species extracted from over 54,000 scientific references. Tailored for researchers, risk assessors, and decision-makers, ECOTOX supports critical environmental protection efforts, including ecological risk assessments, the development of water quality criteria, and chemical prioritization under regulatory frameworks like the Toxic Substances Control Act (TSCA). The database employs rigorous systematic review protocols and adheres to FAIR data principles to ensure high data quality. Furthermore, ECOTOX features advanced search, exploration, and visualization tools, and offers robust interoperability with other computational toxicology resources such as the CompTox Chemicals Dashboard, SeqAPASS, and EcoDrug. By providing essential empirical in vivo data, ECOTOX also plays a pivotal role in advancing New Approach Methodologies (NAMs) and predictive modeling, ultimately reducing reliance on animal testing while enhancing chemical safety evaluations.

## Extended Summary
The Ecotoxicology (ECOTOX) Knowledgebase, developed and maintained by the United States Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), stands as the world's most comprehensive curated repository of single-chemical ecotoxicity data. Evolving from earlier specialized databases (AQUIRE, PHYTOTOX, and TERRETOX), the modern unified ECOTOX system contains over one million test records encompassing more than 12,000 chemicals and 13,000 aquatic and terrestrial species, abstracted from over 54,000 peer-reviewed references. The platform is meticulously curated using rigorous systematic review methodologies that align with FAIR (Findable, Accessible, Interoperable, and Reusable) data principles, ensuring the reliability and consistency required for high-level regulatory and scientific applications.

ECOTOX is designed to serve a diverse user base, including environmental researchers, risk assessors, and policymakers. It provides sophisticated Search, Explore, and Data Visualization features that allow users to precisely filter data by exposure duration, biological endpoints, and test conditions. These capabilities directly support critical regulatory mandates under the Clean Water Act, the Federal Insecticide, Fungicide, and Rodenticide Act (FIFRA), and the Toxic Substances Control Act (TSCA). Specifically, ECOTOX data is foundational for establishing aquatic life benchmarks, developing site-specific water quality criteria, and prioritizing emerging contaminants like PFAS for further evaluation.

Beyond traditional risk assessment, ECOTOX is instrumental in the paradigm shift toward New Approach Methodologies (NAMs) in toxicology. By supplying robust empirical in vivo data, the knowledgebase enables the development, training, and validation of computational models such as Quantitative Structure-Activity Relationships (QSARs) and Species Sensitivity Distributions (SSDs). Furthermore, ECOTOX boasts high interoperability with other advanced EPA computational tools, including the CompTox Chemicals Dashboard, SeqAPASS, and EcoDrug. This seamless integration facilitates cross-species extrapolation and mechanistically-driven ecological risk assessments, significantly reducing the reliance on vertebrate animal testing. Through continuous quarterly updates and comprehensive training programs, ECOTOX remains an indispensable asset for advancing ecological health protection and modernizing chemical safety evaluations.
